## The short version

I'm a full-stack developer based in Dublin. I mostly build with TypeScript, React and Next.js, with Node.js and PostgreSQL on the backend.

I care about two things in particular: <Highlight>getting the logic right</Highlight>, and making the result easy to use. This site is a small example of both.

## Where I am right now

I'm in my final year of a BSc (Hons) in Computing and Information Technology at CCT College Dublin, graduating in May 2027. I'm looking for graduate or junior developer roles starting after that.

In summer 2024 I interned as a software developer, migrating ERP modules from Zend Framework to Laminas and building responsive UI for customer and employee portals. It was my first time working inside a large, long-lived codebase, where every change has to keep things running for the people who depend on it.

## From Santa Catarina to Dublin

I grew up in Rio Negrinho, in the south of Brazil, and moved to Ireland in 2023 to grow as a developer and see a different side of the world. Rio Negrinho is a small inland town known for its furniture factories and cold winters, so the Irish weather was less of a shock than people expect.
